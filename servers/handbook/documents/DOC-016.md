# Shipping service levels: quick reference

## Purpose

This is a short summary of domestic shipping service levels for quick answers at the desk. The full policy is the document titled Domestic shipping service levels, which is authoritative. If this sheet and that document disagree, follow that document.

## At a glance

Standard ground: three to six business days, $9.50, free for orders of $200 or more. Expedited: two business days, $24. Overnight: next business day, $48. Orders over $500 need a signature; residential delivery adds $4. Oversize items longer than 48 inches add $15.

## Cut-off times

Orders placed by 3:00 pm Port Calder time on a business day ship the same day. Overnight orders must be in by 12:30 pm. No weekend shipping.

## Limits

Parcels up to 70 pounds. Heavier goods go by freight. No deliveries to post office boxes. Staff who need exact surcharges, weight rules or tracking steps should open the full document rather than rely on this summary. Tracking numbers are emailed once a parcel is scanned out.

## Common questions

If a customer asks whether a weekend order ships on Monday, the answer is yes, provided it was placed before the Monday cut-off. For orders that miss the cut-off, tell the customer the parcel leaves the next business day. Staff should not promise delivery on a specific day, because carriers set the final scan time. For international orders, use the document on international shipping and customs paperwork. For batteries, cold-chain goods and freight, use the dedicated documents.
