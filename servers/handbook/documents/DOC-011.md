# Wholesale account returns

## Who this covers

This document applies to customers with a wholesale trade account, meaning those with a signed supply agreement and net payment terms. Their returns are handled by the account manager rather than the general returns queue. Marine retailers and chandlers that buy seasonally are the most common wholesale returners, usually in the weeks after the boating season ends in October.

## Eligibility and timing

Wholesale customers may return unsold, unopened cartons within 60 days of delivery. Pallets must be returned whole, with the original shrink wrap. Opened cartons are accepted only if more than 90 percent of the units inside are intact.

## Credit notes

Returns are paid by credit note applied to the account balance, never by card refund. The note is issued within five business days of inspection and can be used on any invoice within 12 months. A credit note cannot be exchanged for cash unless the account is being closed.

## Fees and freight

The returns fee for wholesale accounts is 8 percent of the invoice value, capped at $1,500 per shipment. We arrange collection by freight carrier when the return is worth more than $2,000; below that, the customer pays to ship it back.

## Approvals

The account manager approves returns up to $5,000. Returns above $5,000 need written approval from the head of wholesale. All wholesale returns still require a return authorisation number, issued as described in the RMA process document. Disputes about the credit amount are resolved by the account manager within five business days; unresolved cases go to the head of wholesale.
