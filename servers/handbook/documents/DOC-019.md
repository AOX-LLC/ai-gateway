# Passwords and multi-factor sign-in

## Password rules

Passwords for Harborline accounts must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or five unrelated words is easier to remember than a short string of symbols and is just as strong. Never reuse a work password anywhere else, and never share it with a colleague, including your supervisor or the IT desk. Passwords are changed only when there is a reason, such as a suspected leak, not on a fixed schedule.

## Password manager

Every employee has a licence for the company password manager. Store all work credentials there, let it generate unique passwords, and protect the vault with a long passphrase. Do not keep passwords in spreadsheets, sticky notes or browser autofill on shared computers.

## Multi-factor sign-in

Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the order system, the finance system and remote access. Use the authenticator app on your phone. Text-message codes are accepted only as a short-term fallback. Approve a sign-in prompt only when you started it yourself; an unexpected prompt is a warning that someone has your password, so decline it and tell the IT desk.

## Setting up and recovering

New staff enrol their phone during the first day with IT. If you change phones, transfer the authenticator before wiping the old one. If you are locked out, ask IT for a one-time recovery code, which they issue only after confirming your identity by video call with your supervisor present.

## Warehouse shared terminals

Shared scanners and terminals use a badge tap plus a four-digit PIN. Lock the terminal when you walk away; sessions time out after five minutes of inactivity.
