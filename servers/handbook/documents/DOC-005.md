# Warehouse onboarding checklist

## Before day one

The People team sends a welcome email five days before the start date with the shift time, the Saltmarsh Quay gate to use and a list of what to bring. Photo identification is needed for the badge. Steel-toe boots are required; the company pays a $90 boot allowance after the first month.

## Day one

Arrive 30 minutes before your shift for a badge and locker. A site safety induction of about 90 minutes covers fire exits, spill kits, pedestrian walkways and the forklift exclusion zones. You will be paired with a buddy who stays with you for the first two weeks. Nobody may operate powered equipment on day one. Lockers are issued for the length of your employment and may be inspected with 24 hours' notice. Mobile phones stay in lockers on the pick floor except for scanners issued by the company.

## Week one

Complete the online modules on manual handling, hazard labels and scanner use. Learn the pick-and-pack flow with your buddy, including bin locations and how to scan a pallet label. Sign off the checklist in the staff portal each afternoon.

## Forklift and equipment certification

Forklift and pallet-jack training is a two-day course held monthly. Certification is valid for three years. Only certified staff may drive powered equipment, and a refresher is required after any reported incident.

## 30-60-90 day check-ins

Your supervisor meets you at day 30, day 60 and day 90. Each meeting reviews accuracy, safety record and questions. The 90-day review confirms the end of probation, and the checklist is filed with your record. If you are struggling at any point, tell your buddy or supervisor early; extra training days can be added at no cost to you.
