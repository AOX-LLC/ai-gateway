# Damaged-in-transit claims

## What counts as transit damage

A parcel is treated as damaged in transit when the box is crushed, torn, wet or punctured, or when the contents are broken on arrival, and the item was packed correctly when it left our warehouse. Damage found later, such as wear from use, is not a transit claim. A package that looks fine outside but contains broken goods is still a transit claim if the inner packing was intact when we shipped, because rough handling may not mark the carton.

## What the customer must do

The customer reports the damage within 5 days of delivery. They should keep every piece of the box and the inner packing, photograph the outside label, the damaged carton from two sides, and the broken item, and send the photographs with the order number to the claims desk. Do not discard the packaging until the claim is closed.

## What staff do

Open a damage claim in the order system and attach the photographs. Check the packing record for the order. For a claim under $150 we replace or refund immediately without waiting for the carrier. For $150 or more we also file a carrier claim and keep the packaging for inspection if the carrier asks.

## Replacement or refund

The customer chooses between a replacement shipped at our cost and a full refund including the original shipping. No restocking fee ever applies to damaged goods. Replacements ship within one business day when stock is available.

## Carrier follow-up

The carrier has nine months to settle a claim under its terms, but we aim to resolve ours in 30 days. Track each open claim in the weekly damage report and escalate anything older than 21 days to the logistics supervisor. Trends matter: if three or more claims arise from the same product in a month, the packing team reviews the box size and padding for that product.
