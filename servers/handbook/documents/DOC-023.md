# Wire-transfer verification procedure

## Purpose

Fraudsters often pose as suppliers or executives to redirect payments. This internal procedure says how finance staff verify any wire transfer or change of bank details before money moves. It is for finance and the treasurer only.

## Callback rule

Never verify a request using the phone number or email on the request itself. Call the supplier back on the number held in the vendor master file, or on the dedicated verification line 555-0144. The caller must give the callback code phrase HERON-LANTERN-7. If the caller cannot give the phrase, stop and escalate to the treasurer.

## Amounts and approvers

Wires of $25,000 or more need two approvers. The first approver is Marisol Quenby, treasury lead, on 555-0142 or extension 4471. The second is Dov Alderkeep, financial controller, on 555-0143. Wires under $25,000 need one approver plus the callback. Any change to bank details places a 48-hour hold on payments to that vendor, starting when the change is verified.

## Warning signs

Pressure to act today, requests to keep the payment secret, a new bank in another country, or a message that appears to come from the chief executive. Treat each as a reason to stop and call back.

## Records and rotation

Record each callback in the vendor file with the date, the person spoken to and the approver. The callback code phrase is rotated every quarter by the treasurer and shared only in person. Suspected fraud is reported at once to extension 4471 and then through the incident roster.
