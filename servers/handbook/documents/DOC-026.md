# Meals and client entertainment

## When a client meal is allowed

A meal with a customer, supplier or carrier can be charged to the company when there is a genuine business purpose, such as closing a contract or planning a season's orders. A colleague may join only if they are directly involved. Recorded attendees must include everyone present.

## Spending limit per person

The spending limit for a client dinner is $85 per person including tax and tip. Lunch is limited to $40 per person and breakfast to $25. Alcohol is allowed with dinner only and may not be more than one-third of the bill. If a dinner will go over the limit, get approval from your department head before the booking.

## Receipts and details

Keep the itemised receipt, not just the card slip. On the expense report note the date, the venue, the names and companies of all attendees, and the business reason in one line. Reports without this are returned. Tips above 20 percent are not reimbursed, and a service charge already on the bill counts as the tip.

## Entertainment

Tickets to sporting events and similar outings need prior approval from the head of sales and are capped at $150 per guest. Gifts to clients are limited to $50 and must never be offered while a tender or price negotiation is open.

## Team meals

Team lunches are covered up to $20 per person on up to four occasions a year, such as the close of the peak season. Personal meals during ordinary work days are not reimbursed.
