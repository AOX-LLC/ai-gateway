# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone at Harborline is expected to treat colleagues, customers, carriers and suppliers with respect. We deal honestly, keep our promises, and own our mistakes. This code applies on site, online, at company events and while travelling for work. The code is not a rulebook for every situation. When something is not covered, ask what a fair and careful colleague would do, and check with your supervisor if still unsure.

## Respect and safety

Harassment, bullying, discrimination and threats are not tolerated, whether in person, by message or on social media. Horseplay on the dock, ignoring forklift rules and working under the influence of alcohol or drugs are treated as serious safety breaches. If you see unsafe behaviour, stop the task and tell a supervisor straight away.

## Conflicts of interest and gifts

Tell your supervisor before taking part in any outside business that overlaps with ours or has us as a customer or supplier. You may accept a supplier gift worth up to $50 a year. Anything above that must be declined politely or handed to the People team. Never accept cash or cash equivalents.

## Confidentiality

Customer lists, pricing agreements and staff records are confidential. Share them only with colleagues who need them for their work. The duty continues after you leave the company. Do not post photographs of the warehouse floor, shipping labels or screens on social media, because they can reveal customer names and addresses.

## Raising concerns

You can raise a concern with your supervisor, the People team, or the confidential line at 555-0120. Reports can be anonymous. We do not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. The People team acknowledges every report within two working days and aims to conclude an investigation within 30 days.
