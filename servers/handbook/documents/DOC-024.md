# Incident escalation roster

## When to use this roster

This internal roster says who to call when a security incident is declared, such as a confirmed data leak, ransomware, or unauthorised access to the order system. It is restricted to the response team and department heads.

## Severity levels

Severity 1 is an active compromise or customer data exposure; call within 10 minutes. Severity 2 is a contained incident, such as a single infected laptop; call within one hour. Severity 3 is a suspected but unconfirmed event; log it by the next working day. Record the severity and the time of the call in the incident log, even if the incident is later downgraded.
