# Lost laptop or phone

## Report it immediately

If your work laptop or phone is lost or stolen, call the IT desk on 555-0130 as soon as you notice, at any hour. A voicemail is accepted outside working hours. Do not wait to look for it first; speed matters more than certainty. Also tell your supervisor.

## What IT does

IT will lock the device remotely, sign out all sessions, revoke your authenticator and tokens, and, for devices that hold customer data, wipe it the next time it connects. Disk encryption is mandatory on every laptop, which keeps the data safe in the meantime, but IT still treats the device as compromised.

## Details to give

Say when and where you last had it, whether it was locked, whether it held customer records or saved passwords, and whether other items such as access badges were lost with it. If it was left on a train or in a taxi, contact the transport operator's lost property office too.

## Theft and personal devices

If the device was stolen, file a report with the local police and give IT the reference number. Staff who use a personal phone for work email must also report its loss, because company email on it is wiped remotely.

## Replacement

A replacement laptop is normally issued within one business day. You will not be charged for a first loss reported promptly. Repeated losses, or failing to report within 24 hours, are reviewed by your department head. Do not buy a replacement yourself.
