# Paid time off

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees at Harborline earn paid vacation days that accrue every pay period. In years one to three you receive 15 vacation days. From the start of year four the allowance rises to 20 days, and from year eight it is 25 days. Part-time staff accrue time in proportion to their scheduled hours. New hires may start using accrued days after their first 60 days of employment.

## Requesting time away

Submit vacation requests in the staff portal at least 14 days ahead for absences of up to five working days, and at least 30 days ahead for anything longer. Your supervisor answers within three working days. During peak season, 1 October to 20 December, no more than two people per warehouse shift may be away at once, and requests are approved in the order they arrive.

## Carry-over and payout

You may carry up to five unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Anything above five is forfeited on 31 March unless your department head approves an extension in writing. When employment ends, accrued and unused vacation is paid out at your final hourly rate, up to a cap of 30 days. Requests to carry over more than five days must be submitted before 15 January. Approvals are recorded in the staff portal so that payroll can see the balance.

## Sick days and personal days

Every employee also receives six paid sick days and two personal days each year. Sick days do not carry over. Tell your supervisor before your shift starts if you are unwell. A clinician's note is required only after three consecutive sick days. Personal days need 48 hours' notice except in an emergency.

## Public holidays

The company observes ten paid public holidays, listed each January on the staff portal. Warehouse staff who are rostered on a holiday are paid at 1.5 times their normal rate and receive a replacement day off within 60 days.
