# Travel expenses

## Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the company travel desk at least 14 days ahead where possible. Economy fares are the default. Flights over six hours may be booked in premium economy with department head approval. Rental cars are permitted only when public transport is not practical.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $190 in major cities and $140 elsewhere, including taxes. Choose a hotel within a reasonable distance of the work site. Room upgrades, minibar items, in-room films and spa services are personal costs. Stay no longer than the work requires.

## Daily allowances

Travellers receive a per diem of $65 a day for meals and incidentals on overnight trips, with no receipts needed for the per diem. On the first and last day of travel the allowance is 75 percent. Where a client meal is provided, claim a reduced rate. Tips of up to 15 percent are covered.

## Ground transport

Use airport trains or shuttles first. Taxis and ride-share are reimbursed when they are the cheapest practical option, with receipts attached. Parking at airports is reimbursed up to $30 a day.

## Submitting the claim

Submit the expense report within 30 days of the end of the trip, with itemised receipts for any single expense above $25. Late claims over 60 days old are not paid without department head sign-off. Approval limits are in the expense approval limits document. Receipts may be photographed with a phone and uploaded in the expense app; paper originals need not be kept after the claim is paid.
