# Vendor payment approval thresholds

## Scope

This internal document sets who may release payments to suppliers and service vendors. It is for the finance team and signatories only and covers accounts-payable runs, not staff expense claims.

## Approval tiers

Payments up to $7,500 can be released by one approver, Odalys Brenwick, accounts payable lead, on 555-0161. Payments from $7,501 to $40,000 need two signatures, Odalys Brenwick and the financial controller. Payments from $40,001 to $120,000 also need the chief executive. Anything above $120,000 requires a board resolution. The finance team records the approver names and times in the payment log. Odalys Brenwick may delegate to a named alternate for a maximum of 14 days, in writing.

## Release and codes

Payment batches are released in the banking portal. Emergency out-of-cycle payments require the release code KESTREL-TALLOW-58, given by the finance desk on extension 4620 after a callback. Without the code the batch stays held.

## Controls

The person who enters a vendor may not release its payments. New vendors are held for five business days and checked against the sanctions list. Invoices are matched to purchase orders and goods received before payment. Prepayments and deposits follow the same tiers as invoices and are recorded as separate entries. Recurring payments, such as freight carrier accounts, are approved once a year and then released by the standing run.

## Exceptions and review

Exceptions are approved in writing by the financial controller and logged. The controller reviews all payments above $7,500 each month and the release code is changed every six months.
