# Customer returns policy (2025 edition)

## Return window

Customers may return eligible goods within 30 days of the delivery date shown on the carrier scan. After 30 days we cannot accept a return, except for faulty goods covered by the manufacturer warranty. The window applies equally to opened and unopened items.

## Condition and proof

Items must be clean, complete and in resalable condition, with all packaging and accessories. Special-order goods, cut-to-length rope and chain, and sealed safety items are not returnable unless faulty. Customers must quote the original order number. No photographs are required for a standard return. Customers who are unsure whether an item is eligible should contact customer service with the order number before posting anything back to us.

## Restocking fee

A restocking fee of 15 percent of the item price is deducted from every refund for non-faulty returns. The fee is waived when we shipped the wrong item or the item arrived faulty. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the error was ours.

## Refunds

Once the return reaches the Port Calder returns bay and passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Wholesale accounts receive a credit note instead of a card refund. Credit card refunds may take a further five days to appear on the customer's statement, which is outside our control.

## Return authorisation

Every return needs a return authorisation number before it is shipped. Customer service issues the number. Parcels that arrive without a number are held for 14 days and then returned to the sender at their cost.
