# Domestic shipping service levels

## Service levels

Harborline offers three domestic service levels within the continental United States. Standard ground takes three to six business days and costs a flat $9.50 for orders under $200, free above. Expedited takes two business days and costs $24. Overnight delivers the next business day and costs $48. Prices are for the continental United States only. Alaska, Hawaii and territories are quoted separately.

## Order cut-off times

Orders placed by 2:00 pm Port Calder time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders after 2:00 pm leave the next business day. Overnight orders must be placed by 12:30 pm to make the same-day pickup. Orders are not shipped on weekends or on company holidays.

## Weight and size limits

Parcels may weigh up to 70 pounds and measure up to 108 inches in length plus girth. Anything heavier goes by freight on a pallet and is quoted separately. Freight quotes are valid for seven days. Items longer than 48 inches are treated as oversize and add a $15 handling charge to parcel services.

## Delivery address rules

We ship to street addresses only. Parcels cannot be sent to post office boxes. Residential deliveries add a $4 surcharge. Signature on delivery is required for orders above $500 at no extra cost.

## Tracking and delays

A tracking number is emailed when the parcel is scanned out. If a parcel has no movement for three business days, open a trace with the carrier. This document is the authoritative statement of service levels and cut-off times; the quick-reference sheet is only a summary.
