# Mileage reimbursement

## When mileage is paid

Staff who use their own car for company business are reimbursed for the business miles. Examples include driving to a customer, a supplier or the airport for a work trip. Commuting between home and your usual workplace is not business mileage.

## The rate

For 2026 the mileage rate is $0.62 per mile for the use of a personal car. Staff who drive a personal van loaded with company goods receive $0.70 per mile. The rate covers fuel, insurance, wear and depreciation, so you cannot also claim fuel or repairs. Finance reviews the rate each January. Electric and hybrid cars are paid the same rate as other cars.

## Recording the trip

Keep a mileage log with the date, start and end points, purpose and the miles driven. Use the distance shown on the online map tool for the shortest sensible route. Where the trip begins from home, claim only the miles beyond your normal commute. Round to the nearest mile. If you travel with a colleague in the same car, only the driver claims mileage. Trips that combine business and personal stops are claimed only for the business portion of the distance.

## Parking and tolls

Tolls and parking on a business trip are reimbursed in addition to mileage with receipts. Fines and speeding tickets are never reimbursed.

## Insurance and limits

You must hold valid car insurance that covers business use. Claims over 1,000 miles in a month need your supervisor's review. Submit mileage within 30 days of the trip using the expense app.
