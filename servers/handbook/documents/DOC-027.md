# Corporate card use

## Who gets a card

Corporate cards are issued to staff who regularly buy on the company's behalf, such as buyers, sales managers and the logistics team. Cards are issued after a short training session. The card is yours alone and must not be lent to anyone.

## Permitted use

Use the card for business expenses within policy: travel, client meals, supplies and software subscriptions. Do not use it for personal purchases, even if you plan to repay. Cash withdrawals are blocked. The default monthly limit is $3,000, and a temporary increase needs finance approval. Fuel for company vehicles has its own fuel card, which is separate from this one. Subscriptions over $200 a year need your supervisor's approval before purchase.

## Receipts and reconciliation

Upload a photo of the receipt to the expense app within five working days of each purchase. Reconcile all transactions by the 5th of the following month. Cards of staff with missing receipts after 10 days are suspended until the paperwork is complete.

## Lost or misused cards

Report a lost or stolen card on the number printed on the back and tell finance the same day. Suspected fraudulent charges are disputed within 30 days. Personal charges made by mistake must be repaid within 10 days. Deliberate misuse is a conduct matter. Card statements are available in the expense app on the first working day of each month, so check them promptly.

## Leaving the company

Return your card to finance on your last day. All outstanding receipts must be submitted before final pay is released.
