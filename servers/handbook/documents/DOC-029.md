# Expense approval limits

## Why limits exist

Approval limits make sure that spending is checked by someone who understands it, and that nobody approves their own claims. These limits apply to employee expense reports, not to supplier invoices.

## Who approves what

Expense reports totalling up to $500 are approved by your direct supervisor. Reports from $500.01 to $2,500, including a report of $2,000, need your department head. Reports from $2,500.01 to $10,000 need the head of finance. Anything above $10,000 needs the chief executive. The total of the report decides the level, not any single line. For example, a $2,000 conference report goes to your department head, and a $650 report of client meals goes to the same person because it is above $500.

## Splitting is not allowed

Do not divide a large spend across several reports or cards to stay under a limit. Finance reviews patterns each month and splitting is treated as a conduct matter. Corporate card spending counts toward the report total, because it is part of the same expense claim.

## Self and peer approval

You may never approve your own report. Supervisors' reports go to their own department head, and department heads' reports go to the head of finance. Approvers confirm the business purpose, the receipts and the policy limits before they sign.

## Timing

Approvers should act within five working days. Reports waiting longer are escalated automatically to the next level. Approved reports are paid on the second Friday of the month. During the holiday period, approvers name a backup, and the portal routes the report to that person.
