# Remote and hybrid work

## Which roles can work remotely

Office-based roles in customer service, finance, purchasing and marketing may work from home on a hybrid schedule. Warehouse, dispatch and dock roles must work on site because the work cannot be done remotely. Your supervisor decides which pattern fits your role.

## Standard hybrid pattern

The standard hybrid arrangement is up to three days a week at home and at least two days in the office. Everyone on a team should share the same two core office days, Tuesday and Thursday, so that meetings and training happen face to face. Working from home two days a week is available to all eligible staff once they have finished their 90-day probation. Eligible staff who want a different pattern, such as four days at home, may propose one to their department head with a short business case covering coverage and team meetings.

## Applying and changing arrangements

Request a hybrid schedule through the staff portal. Your supervisor responds within five working days. Arrangements are reviewed every six months and may be changed with 14 days' notice if the business need changes. Temporary full-remote weeks, such as when travelling, need approval from your department head.

## Equipment and expenses

Harborline supplies a laptop, a headset and one monitor. A home-office stipend of $300 is paid once, and a monthly internet allowance of $25 is paid to staff working remotely at least two days a week. Furniture is not reimbursed. Company devices must be used for company work and kept on the approved security settings.

## Availability and workspace

Be reachable between 9:30 am and 3:30 pm local time on workdays. Work from a private space where client details cannot be seen or overheard. Do not work from public places on devices that show customer records unless a privacy screen is fitted. If your home connection fails, tell your supervisor by 9:30 am and move to the office or a tethered phone connection until it is fixed.
