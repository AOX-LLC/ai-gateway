# Reporting a phishing email

## Spotting a suspicious message

Phishing messages try to rush you into clicking a link, opening an attachment or sharing a password. Warning signs include a sender address that looks almost right, an unexpected invoice, a demand for urgent payment, a login page reached through a link, and spelling that does not match the sender's usual style.

## If you have not clicked

Do not open attachments or click links. Use the Report Phish button in the mail client, which sends a copy to the security team and removes the message from your inbox. If there is no button, forward the message as an attachment to phishing@harborline.example. Then delete it.

## If you clicked a link or opened a file

Act fast and do not feel embarrassed; quick reporting limits harm. Disconnect from the network by turning off wifi or unplugging the cable, but leave the computer on. Call the IT desk on 555-0130 straight away and tell them what you clicked and when. If you typed a password on the page, change it from another device and tell IT so they can reset your sessions. Do not try to clean up the machine yourself.

## What happens next

The security team checks whether the same message reached others, blocks the sender and the link, and scans your device. You will normally hear back within four hours during working hours. Most incidents are closed the same day.

## Why it matters

A single stolen password can expose customer accounts and payment details. We treat every report as helpful, and nobody is disciplined for reporting a mistake promptly. Failing to report is the part that causes trouble.
