# Performance review cycle

## Annual rhythm

Harborline runs one formal performance review each year. Goals are agreed in February, a check-in happens in July, and the full review is completed in November. Pay changes from the review take effect on 1 January. Reviews are scheduled by the People team, which sends calendar invitations three weeks in advance. Employees who joined after 1 September are reviewed the following year, though they still receive a 90-day review.

## Self-assessment and feedback

Two weeks before the review, you complete a self-assessment in the staff portal covering results against your goals, strengths and development needs. Your supervisor also gathers brief feedback from up to three colleagues. Feedback is summarised rather than quoted by name.

## The review meeting

The meeting lasts 45 minutes. Your supervisor shares a rating on a four-point scale: Exceeds, Meets, Partly meets, or Below. Ratings are discussed with the department head for consistency before they are given. You may add written comments within 10 working days. Bring examples of your work, such as picking accuracy figures or customer thanks, so that the conversation uses facts rather than impressions.

## Development and improvement plans

Every review ends with at least two development actions, such as a course or a stretch project. A rating of Below leads to a 60-day improvement plan with weekly check-ins and clear measures. Most plans end with the employee back on track.

## Disagreeing with a rating

If you disagree with your rating, ask the People team for a second-level review within 15 working days. A different department head reads the evidence and replies within 10 working days. That decision is final.
