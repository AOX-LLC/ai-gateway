# Visitor access to warehouses

## Sign in and badges

Every visitor to the Port Calder and Saltmarsh Quay warehouses signs in at the gatehouse, shows photo identification and receives a visitor badge, which must be worn on the chest at all times. Contractors who work on site for more than one day need an induction and a contractor badge instead.

## Hosts

Each visitor needs a named host who has booked them in at least one working day ahead. The host meets the visitor at the gatehouse and stays with them for the whole visit. Visitors are never left alone in the pick area, the returns bay or the stock room. Deliveries by drivers stay within the dock office and the marked yard.

## Safety equipment

Visitors must wear a high-visibility vest, and closed shoes are required. Steel-toe boots and hard hats are provided at the gatehouse for anyone entering the racking aisles. Visitors may not use phones to photograph labels, screens or customer goods without the host's permission.

## Restricted areas

The cage for high-value goods, the server room and the returns inspection area are off limits to visitors. Fire exits must never be used to bring a visitor in or out.

## Leaving and emergencies

The host walks the visitor back to the gatehouse, collects the badge and signs them out. In a fire alarm, hosts take their visitors to the assembly point at the north car park and report to the gatehouse warden. Lost visitor badges are reported to security at once, and unreturned badges are disabled within an hour.
