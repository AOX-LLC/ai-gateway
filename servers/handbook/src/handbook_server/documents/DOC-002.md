# Parental leave

## Who qualifies

Any employee who has worked at Harborline for at least 180 days is eligible for paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child, including foster placement with a view to adoption. The benefit applies equally to birth parents and non-birth parents, and to employees in every department.

## Length and pay

Birth parents receive 14 weeks of leave, with the first 10 weeks paid at 100 percent of base pay and the last four weeks at 60 percent. Non-birth parents and adoptive parents receive 8 weeks, with the first 6 weeks at full pay and the remaining two at 60 percent. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement and may be taken in one block or split into two blocks.

## Giving notice

Tell your supervisor and the People team as early as you reasonably can, ideally 60 days before the expected date. If the baby arrives early, notify us within 10 days. The People team will confirm your dates, pay schedule and benefits in writing within five working days. If you are unsure which category you fall into, ask the People team before you give notice; they will confirm eligibility the same day. Medical appointments before the birth are covered by ordinary sick days and do not use up parental leave.

## Benefits during leave

Health coverage continues on the usual terms while you are away. Vacation and sick time keep accruing during paid weeks but not during the reduced-pay weeks. Your shift pattern and role are protected, and you return to the same job or an equivalent one.

## Returning to work

A phased return is available. For the first four weeks back you may work 80 percent of your hours at full pay. A private lactation room is set aside at the Port Calder warehouse and the head office, and flexible break times are available on request. Talk to your supervisor about a return plan two weeks before your leave ends.
