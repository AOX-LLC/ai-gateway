# Customer returns policy (2026 edition)

## Return window and what this edition replaces

This edition supersedes the 2025 edition of the customer returns policy for all orders delivered on or after 1 February 2026. Orders delivered before that date continue under the earlier rules. Customers may return unopened items in their original sealed packaging within 45 days of the delivery date shown on the carrier scan. Opened items may be returned within 30 days. After those periods we cannot accept a return, except for faulty goods covered by the manufacturer warranty. To work out the window, count calendar days from the carrier delivery scan, not the order date. Day one is the day after delivery.

## Condition and proof

Items must be clean, complete and in resalable condition, with all packaging and accessories. Special-order goods, cut-to-length rope and chain, and sealed safety items are not returnable unless faulty. Customers must quote the original order number and, new in this edition, attach at least two photographs showing the item and its packaging before a return authorisation is issued. Photographs should be clear, well lit and show any labels or serial numbers. Customer service may ask for extra images when the first set is unclear, and the 45-day clock does not stop while waiting for them.

## Restocking fee

A restocking fee of 10 percent of the item price is deducted from every refund for non-faulty returns. The fee is waived when we shipped the wrong item or the item arrived faulty. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the error was ours.

## Refunds

Once the return reaches the Port Calder returns bay and passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within seven business days. Wholesale accounts receive a credit note instead of a card refund.

## Return authorisation

Every return needs a return authorisation number before it is shipped. Customer service issues the number. Parcels that arrive without a number are held for 14 days and then returned to the sender at their cost.
